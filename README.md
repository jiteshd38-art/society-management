🏢 Society Management System

🚀 A Smart & Efficient Solution for Modern Society Management

A modern, user-friendly platform designed to simplify and automate residential society operations. It provides a centralized system for managing residents, maintenance, complaints, visitors, and announcements, ensuring transparency, security, and seamless communication.

✨ Key Features

- 👥 Resident Management – Easily manage resident profiles and apartment details.
- 💰 Maintenance Management – Track maintenance bills, payments, and dues.
- 📝 Complaint Management – Register, monitor, and resolve resident complaints.
- 🔐 Visitor Management – Maintain visitor records and enhance society security.
- 📢 Notice Board – Share important announcements, notices, and updates.
- 🏠 Flat Management – Organize and manage flats and occupancy details.
- 📊 Admin Dashboard – Monitor and manage society activities from one place.
- 🔔 Notifications & Alerts – Keep residents informed about important updates.
- 🔑 Secure Authentication – Provide role-based access for admins and residents.

🎯 Project Objective

To create a smart, secure, and paperless society management solution that improves efficiency, reduces manual work, and enhances the overall residential living experience.
